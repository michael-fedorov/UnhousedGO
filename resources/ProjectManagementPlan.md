This is the project plan.

We are creating a mobile application to be used by homeless residents of Glendale. Use the Glendale GIS API in resources/GISmap.md for . The aim of the application is to provide an application resource for homeless people to find relevant resources near them. Examples of resources for the homeless are public restrooms, clean water, showers, internet/technology/outlet access (e.g. libraries), and shelter/sleeping locations (homeless shelters, churches that offer places to sleep). If available, all resources should have their opening and closing times available.

We may also want to provide important disaster info that homeless people would need in particular. 

The app should be similar in appearance to the app Pokemon GO, which is an app with a map following your current location and including "pokestops", or locations of special interest (resource points).

App name: Unhoused GO
